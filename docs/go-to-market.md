# Go-To-Market Plan

## Initial Offer

### 25-Account Pipeline Sprint

For consultants, small agencies, and founder-led B2B teams.

Deliverables:

- 25 researched account briefs
- fit score and evidence for each account
- suggested outreach angle
- first follow-up draft for approved accounts
- stale-opportunity review if prior context is provided
- weekly analytics report
- next-sprint recommendations

Positioning:

"I help you turn a rough target list into an evidence-backed follow-up queue and a pipeline report, without handing your reputation to an autonomous outbound bot."

## Why This Offer Should Convert

- Clear before/after.
- Does not require CRM migration.
- Does not require trusting an agent to send messages.
- Works as a one-off service.
- Can become recurring if it saves time or improves replies.

## First Niche Options

### AI/Automation Consultants

Pros:

- understand the pain
- willing to experiment
- likely to value workflow quality

Cons:

- may try to build it themselves

### Small B2B Agencies

Pros:

- recurring prospecting need
- high value if meetings convert
- often operationally messy

Cons:

- may have weak ICPs
- may expect cheap lead lists

### Local High-Ticket Services

Examples:

- commercial cleaning
- managed IT
- immigration consultants
- accounting/tax firms
- specialty clinics

Pros:

- clear local/account research angle
- less saturated than SaaS outbound

Cons:

- discovery takes more manual context

## Recommended First ICP

Start with small B2B agencies and consultants selling high-ticket services.

Reason:

- they already need leads and follow-ups
- the buyer is reachable
- they can pay for one sprint
- quality matters more than volume

## Pricing

### Starter Audit

Price: $250-$500

Scope:

- review current pipeline workflow
- score 5 sample accounts
- draft 2 follow-ups
- identify workflow gaps

### Pipeline Sprint

Price: $1,000-$2,500

Scope:

- 25-50 target accounts
- lead briefs
- follow-up drafts
- analytics report
- 45-minute review call

### Monthly Operator

Price: $1,500-$5,000/month

Scope:

- weekly research batch
- follow-up queue
- analytics readout
- workflow improvements

## Outreach Message

Use this only as a human-edited starting point.

```text
Subject: quick pipeline ops idea

Hey {{name}},

I am testing a small pipeline sprint for agencies/consultants that already have a target list but lose time on research and follow-up discipline.

The output is not automated spam. It is 25 evidence-backed account briefs, human-approved follow-up drafts, and a short analytics report showing where the pipeline is getting stuck.

If useful, I can do a 3-account sample for {{company}} and show you the format.

Franklin
```

## Proof Assets To Build

1. Sample 25-account report with fake/safe companies.
2. Before/after lead brief example.
3. Follow-up approval queue screenshot.
4. Weekly analytics report screenshot.
5. Short video walkthrough.
6. Public GitHub repo showing workflow-pack design.

## Sales Learning Questions

Ask every prospect:

- Where do leads currently come from?
- What makes a lead worth pursuing?
- Where do follow-ups fall through?
- What tools already hold the context?
- What would make a researched lead brief trustworthy?
- What would make this not worth paying for?

## Conversion Criteria

A prospect is a good fit if:

- they sell a high-value service or B2B product
- they can define a target customer
- they have some lead source or target list
- they care about quality over mass volume
- they are willing to review messages before sending

A prospect is a bad fit if:

- they want bulk scraping
- they want fully automated sending
- they cannot define their offer
- they measure only email volume
- they expect guaranteed meetings from cold outbound

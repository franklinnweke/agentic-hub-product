# Service Offer: Agentic Hub Pipeline Sprint

## Headline

Turn a rough target list into evidence-backed lead briefs, human-reviewed follow-up drafts, and a weekly pipeline report without autonomous outbound automation.

## Who It Is For

Agentic Hub is for consultants, boutique agencies, founder-led B2B teams, and high-ticket service businesses that already have some lead source but lack a consistent research, follow-up, and analytics workflow.

Best-fit customers:

- sell a high-value service or B2B product
- can describe a target customer
- care about quality over outreach volume
- are willing to review messages before sending
- want an inspectable workflow rather than a black-box sales bot

Bad-fit customers:

- want bulk scraping
- want autonomous sending
- cannot define their offer
- measure only email volume
- need platform-risky automation

## Offer

### 25-Account Pipeline Sprint

Franklin runs a supervised local workflow over a curated target list and returns a reviewed pipeline operating pack.

Deliverables:

- imported and normalized target list
- evidence-backed account briefs
- transparent fit scoring
- missing-information and disqualifier flags
- suggested outreach angles
- human-review follow-up drafts using local contact and prior-interaction context when provided
- approval queue state
- weekly analytics report
- static local operator console
- next-sprint recommendations

### Problem

Most small teams do not need another CRM. They need a repeatable way to turn scattered target lists, notes, and context into reviewed action.

Without a workflow, follow-ups slip, lead quality is hard to compare, and generic AI drafts create reputational risk.

### What You Get

- Lead briefs that explain why each account is or is not worth pursuing.
- Drafts that use only available evidence, local contact context, and local prior-interaction notes before starting in human review.
- Analytics that show throughput, review status, draft type mix, manual outcomes, and bottlenecks.
- Local artifacts you can inspect: CSV, JSON, Markdown, HTML, and audit logs.

### What It Will Not Do

- It will not send emails for you.
- It will not submit forms.
- It will not scrape private systems.
- It will not bypass platform controls.
- It will not fake personalization.

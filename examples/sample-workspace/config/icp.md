# ICP: Local Operator Pipeline Sprint

## Ideal Customer

- Solo consultants, boutique agencies, founder-led B2B teams, or small service businesses.
- Teams with relationship-heavy sales motion and high-trust offers.
- Operators already using spreadsheets, docs, inboxes, or lightweight CRMs.
- Buyers who care about evidence quality, follow-up discipline, and weekly operating cadence.

## Disqualifiers

- Requests for autonomous mass outbound.
- Need to bypass platform limits, CAPTCHAs, paywalls, or private systems.
- No clear offer or no legitimate business reason to contact prospects.
- Highly regulated outreach where legal review is required before drafting.

## Scoring Criteria

- ICP match: 30
- Pain signal: 20
- Ability to pay: 15
- Reachable buyer/context: 15
- Timing signal: 10
- Strategic relevance: 10

## Tone

Direct, specific, non-hype, respectful, and low-pressure. Use one clear ask and cite only evidence present in the workspace.

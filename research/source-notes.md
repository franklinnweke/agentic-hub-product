# Source Notes And Research Signals

This repo is based on Franklin's prior research brief about practical Codex and agentic workflow opportunities.

## Strong Signals

- Practical agent value clusters around supervised workflows: QA, repo cleanup, document/data extraction, follow-up drafting, analytics, and repeatable internal tools.
- Community discussions repeatedly warn that unsupervised agents fail silently unless workflows include planning, state, review, and verification.
- Browser/computer use is useful, but best treated as a fallback when APIs or exports are unavailable.
- Codex is well-suited as a workflow workbench: repo-aware work, skills, browser/computer use, GitHub review, automation, and repeatable instructions.
- Productized services are the fastest path to cashflow before SaaS.

## Product Implications

- Sell artifacts and outcomes, not "AI agents."
- Make human approval a core feature.
- Make evidence visible.
- Use analytics to prove value.
- Package repeated work as reusable workflow packs.

## Boundaries

Do not build:

- autonomous spam outreach
- fake personalization engines
- LinkedIn automation that risks account bans
- scraper-first lead farms
- form submission bots
- generic AI email generators

Build:

- evidence-backed lead research
- human-approved draft queues
- follow-up reminders
- pipeline analytics
- reusable Codex skills
- n8n orchestration for scheduled safe workflows
